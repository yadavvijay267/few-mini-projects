Data Analyst — E-Commerce Analytics Team

We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our e-commerce analytics team.
You will turn transaction data into decisions: build dashboards, run
segmentation analyses, and present findings to marketing and product teams.

Required skills:
- Strong Python and SQL for querying and analysing large datasets.
- Hands-on experience with pandas and data analysis.
- Statistics fundamentals: distributions, hypothesis testing, A/B tests.
- Data visualisation: clear charts and dashboards that non-technical
  stakeholders can read.
- Good communication and problem solving skills.

Nice to have:
- Machine learning with scikit-learn.
- Experience with Excel, Git, Docker or AWS.
- Natural language processing (NLP) for customer feedback analysis.
