📷 Face recognition

Real face recognition needs a camera + a recognition model (face_recognition/dlib or OpenCV, ~500 MB of native libraries). This demo simulates the flow: press Simulate face scan and the system "recognises" an enrolled employee with a confidence score. In production, only a 128-d numeric embedding is stored locally — never a photo — and any employee can delete their face data on request.
