How it works

Total loss = length × attenuation + splices × splice loss + connectors × connector loss + margin. Attenuation: SM 1310 nm 0.35 dB/km, SM 1550 nm 0.22 dB/km, MM 850 nm 3.0 dB/km, MM 1300 nm 1.0 dB/km. Power budget = Tx power − Rx sensitivity. The link passes when total loss ≤ power budget.
