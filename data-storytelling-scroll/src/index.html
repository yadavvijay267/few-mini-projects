The Rise of Renewable Energy

How wind and solar went from a rounding error to a third of the world's electricity — in one generation.

Scroll ↓

2000: a niche, mostly water

In 2000 renewables supplied about 20% of global electricity — and almost all of it was hydropower. Wind and solar were barely visible.

2010: wind and solar arrive

By 2010 wind had reached 1.3% and solar 0.4%. Small numbers — but both were doubling every few years.

2018: the crossover decade

Wind (4.8%) plus solar (3.6%) now beat hydropower's growth. New wind and solar capacity outpaced new fossil capacity.

2024: renewables pass 30%

By 2024 renewables supplied roughly 34% of global electricity: hydro 16%, wind 8%, solar 7%, other 2.5%.

What comes next

If wind and solar keep their current pace, they alone could pass hydropower within a decade. The story is no longer "if" — it is "how fast".
