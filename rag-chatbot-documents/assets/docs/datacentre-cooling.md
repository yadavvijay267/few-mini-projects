# Data Centre Cooling

A data hall uses hot-aisle / cold-aisle containment. Cold air is supplied to the cold aisle at 18-27 C and hot air is extracted from the hot aisle. CRAC (computer room air conditioning) units cool and recirculate the air. The recommended inlet temperature for servers is 18 to 27 degrees Celsius, with relative humidity between 20 and 80 percent. If a CRAC unit fails, the runbook for CRAC failure must be started within 5 minutes and the affected racks monitored for temperature rise. A temperature above 32 C at a server inlet is an alert condition.
