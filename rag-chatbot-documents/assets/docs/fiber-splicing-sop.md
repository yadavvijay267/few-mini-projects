# Fiber Splicing SOP

Before splicing, clean the fiber ends with isopropyl alcohol and inspect them under a microscope. A fusion splice should show an estimated loss below 0.05 dB. Protect every splice in a splice tray with a heat-shrink sleeve. Record the splice loss, the tray number, and the closure ID in the asset register. A splice closure in a manhole must be sealed and tested for water ingress before backfilling.
