# Incident Response

When an incident is declared, the on-call engineer opens the relevant runbook, records the start time, and notifies the NOC. Severity 1 incidents (complete outage) require a bridge call within 15 minutes and updates every 30 minutes. All actions are written to the audit log with timestamps. After resolution, a post-incident review is held within 5 working days and the runbook is updated with lessons learned.
