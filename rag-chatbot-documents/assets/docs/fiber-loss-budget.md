# Fiber Loss Budget

Single-mode fiber (OS2) has an attenuation of about 0.35 dB/km at 1310 nm and 0.22 dB/km at 1550 nm. A typical loss budget for a 10 km single-mode link at 1550 nm allows 2.2 dB of fiber loss plus connector and splice losses. Each connector adds about 0.3 dB of loss and each fusion splice adds about 0.05 dB. The total loss budget must stay below the optical power budget of the transceiver, which for a 10GBASE-LR module is about 6 dB. Always measure the installed link with an OTDR and record the trace before handover.

Multimode fiber (OM4) has higher attenuation, about 3.0 dB/km at 850 nm, and is used for short links inside data centres, typically under 550 m for 10 Gb/s.
