Data Centre Operations Guide. The PUE target for the Noida data hall is 1.4 or lower.
PUE is total facility energy divided by IT equipment energy. Cooling uses chilled water
with N+1 CRAC units. The UPS runtime on battery is 15 minutes at full load, and the
generator starts within 30 seconds of a mains failure. Rack power is capped at 8 kW per
rack. Hot aisle containment keeps the cold aisle at 22 degrees Celsius.
Incident response: for a UPS on battery event, confirm generator start, check battery
runtime, and notify the NOC within 5 minutes. For a CRAC failure, open the hot aisle
doors and shift load if the temperature rises above 27 degrees.
